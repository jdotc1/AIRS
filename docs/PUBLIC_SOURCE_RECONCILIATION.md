## Evidence boundaries

The edits neutralize comments, sample labels and internal donor/branch references.
Legal copyright and third-party notices are preserved. Historical review-profile
source receives the already-qualified portable temporary-directory getter. Python
detector/fixture literal construction preserves executable AST and exact test inputs.

Changed source objects and regenerated archives receive publication inspection.
Unchanged Git objects and accepted app build/test/signing/notarization/GUI receipts
are reused. Original qualification records are not rewritten to imply fresh testing
of cleaned historical source. A mapped source identity does not establish byte
identity, successful compilation of every old tag or reproducibility of a signed app.

Release notes distinguish current source metadata from historical publication notes.
The reconciliation supplement updates source lineage and archive checksums without
changing app archives, installed apps, preferences or stores. Current branch/tag
updates do not guarantee erasure of provider caches or previous pull-request refs.
